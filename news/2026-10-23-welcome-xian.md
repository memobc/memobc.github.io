Welcome to [Xian Li](/people/li.html), who has joined the lab as a postdoc! Xian completed a Ph.D. in Psychological and Brain Sciences at Johns Hopkins University in August 2026, where she examined how memory works in naturalistic settings. Xian will be working jointly with the MemoLab and the Affective Brain Sciences Lab to study how memories shape the emotions and social meaning we construct from new experiences. Welcome, Xian!
