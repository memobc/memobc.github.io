#### Education

I am a PhD student in the MemoLab studying how dynamic cognitive states such as emotion shape memory formation and retrieval. I am particularly interested in using naturalistic stimuli to study narrative memories and how they may be related to real-life emotional autobiographical memories.\
\
Previously, I worked on projects related to sleep, hippocampal replay, and mental health as a research specialist in the Schapiro Lab at the University of Pennsylvania. Before then, I graduated with a double major in Neuroscience and Spanish from Duke University, where I completed an undergraduate thesis on motivational influences on memory as part of the Adcock Lab.\
\
Outside the lab, you can find me exploring different farmers markets, trying to find the best matcha cafés, or reading a good book!
